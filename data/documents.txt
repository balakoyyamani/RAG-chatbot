Python is a high-level programming language known for its simplicity and readability. It is widely used in web development, data science, machine learning, and automation.

Machine learning is a branch of artificial intelligence that enables computers to learn from data without being explicitly programmed. It is used in recommendation systems, fraud detection, and image recognition.

Artificial intelligence is the simulation of human intelligence in machines. AI includes machine learning, natural language processing, computer vision, and robotics.

A vector database stores data as numerical vectors called embeddings. It allows semantic search by comparing the similarity between vectors instead of matching exact keywords.

Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) combines a language model with a retrieval system. The retriever finds relevant documents, and the language model generates answers using the retrieved context.

Natural Language Processing (NLP) is a field of AI that enables computers to understand, interpret, and generate human language. Examples include chatbots, translation, and text summarization.

Embeddings are dense numerical representations of text, images, or other data. Similar pieces of information have embeddings that are close together in vector space.

Chunking is the process of splitting large documents into smaller pieces before generating embeddings. Overlapping chunks help preserve context between adjacent sections.

Large Language Models (LLMs) are neural networks trained on massive datasets. They can answer questions, summarize text, generate code, and assist with many language tasks.

Semantic search retrieves information based on meaning rather than exact keyword matching. It uses vector embeddings to identify the most relevant documents.
